# ACCELE ELECTRONICS
### Mobile app • automotive electronics, catalog & client demo guide

A polished, phone-first way to explore Accele Electronics, browse 19 automotive equipment categories, view technical specifications, manage quotes and orders, access 12V installer resources, and submit dealer inquiries.

---

### OPEN THE APP

[Open the Accele Electronics App](https://accele.pages.dev/)  
*The link above opens the app directly. Use the Home, Products, Cart, Orders, and Account sections to move through the main experience.*

---

### APP EXPERIENCE

#### Home
A sleek, automotive-inspired welcome introduces Accele’s 30+ year heritage in mobile video, safety, and 12V electronics. Featured products, quick categories, sub-brand portfolios (*Beuler, AcceleVision, Optix, Littworks, Zicom, USA Pro*), and the Installer & Dealer Hub guide users into shopping, technical guides, or dealer applications.

#### Order / Catalog
The catalog brings together Accele’s complete commercial and consumer product lines across 19 specialized automotive categories. Each listing includes the official part number (SKU), descriptive name, pricing, star rating, and high-resolution product photography.  
Users can search across all items in real time, sort by price or popularity, and filter by categories—from backup cameras and ADAS radar systems to relays, harnesses, seat heaters, and commercial DVR systems.

#### Product Details
Each product features dedicated technical specifications, OE-grade fitment highlights, wiring harness compatibility, in-depth feature lists, and verified customer reviews. In-stock products can be added directly to the cart with real-time subtotal calculation.

#### Installer & Dealer Hub
Designed specifically for professional 12V installers, auto restylers, and fleet managers:
- **Wiring & Pinout Guides:** Quick access to schematics, installation manuals, and technical support.
- **2026 Master Product Catalog:** Direct access to download the comprehensive Accele PDF catalog.
- **Dealer & Wholesale Portal:** An integrated B2B application form allowing prospective dealers to submit company credentials and tax IDs for wholesale pricing.

#### Orders
Users can view order status in real time, filtering between All, Active, Shipped, and Delivered orders. Each entry displays order reference codes, date, items, shipping details, and delivery progress tracking.

#### Support & Resources
The dedicated Support screen provides access to product user manuals, step-by-step installation guides, frequently asked questions, and direct contact avenues for Accele technical support.

### ORDER FLOW

1. **Browse the catalog.** Open Products, search for any part name or SKU (e.g., `AC-5100`, `5035`, `BSS200`), and optionally narrow the list with category filters.
2. **Review product specs.** Tap any product card to view detailed automotive features, specs, and gallery imagery.
3. **Add to cart.** Select quantity and add available items. The floating cart badge and subtotal update instantly.
4. **Choose shipping & fulfillment.** Open Cart and proceed to checkout. Enter delivery destination and choose Standard Shipping (Free) or Express 12V Dispatch ($15.00).
5. **Select payment.** Choose credit/debit card or PayPal demo payment.
6. **Review.** Confirm items, quantities, fulfillment method, shipping address, and final total shown.
7. **Submit and keep the receipt.** Submit the order request, view the celebratory confetti animation, and note the confirmation reference displayed in the app.

---

### PAYMENT & RECEIPT

The app experience is configured for a client demonstration. A successful test checkout leads to an on-screen order confirmation with order reference code, delivery address, and payment summary. Test mode simulates real-world transaction flows without processing live financial charges.

This demonstration uses a streamlined client-side state architecture. Order and payment status reconciliation, ERP inventory sync, commercial payment gateway keys, and live dealer discount schedules can be connected before production deployment.

---

### DEALER & B2B INQUIRY FLOW

1. **Open Dealer Portal in Hub.** Tap "Dealer & Wholesale Portal" from the home screen Hub.
2. **Enter business credentials.** Provide Business/Shop name, contact person, phone number, and commercial email address.
3. **Select account tier.** Choose account classification (*Professional 12V Installer, Auto Dealership / Restyler, Commercial Fleet Operator, or Retailer*).
4. **Review and submit.** Tap "Submit Dealer Application" to receive immediate on-screen submission verification.
5. **Keep the application reference.** An Accele commercial sales representative confirms application details within 24 business hours.
